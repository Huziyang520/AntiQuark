# Anti-Quark

[**简体中文**](#chinese)

## 🇬🇧 English

Minecraft features and gameplay mechanics may sometimes behave in ways that seem counterintuitive or differ from what you would normally expect. **Anti-Quark** introduces a collection of new features, gameplay mechanics, and configuration options that challenge these expectations.

Features that the author believes most players will find unobjectionable are enabled by default. You can also customize the configuration to suit your own preferences.

### ✨ Features

Only a selection is shown below.

- **Wolf Targets Skeleton Horses** — untamed wolves treat skeleton horses as prey.
- **Fearless Cats** — 30% of naturally spawned cats no longer flee from players.
- **Void Totem** — a held totem of undying saves you from the void and lands you somewhere safe.
- **Rainbow Wool** — shearing a sheep named `jeb_` drops wool whose colour keeps shifting.
- **Mining While Riding** — mining from a boat or a minecart is as fast as mining on foot.
- **Leash Any Mob** — survival-mode leads attach to any mob.

The complete list, with defaults and configurable chances, is in **[FEATURES.md](https://github.com/Huziyang520/AntiQuark/blob/master/FEATURES.md)**.

### 📦 Requirements

- Requires [Zeta](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Zeta).

### ⚠️ Notes

- Installing Anti-Quark alongside [Quark](https://github.com/VazkiiMods/Quark) will not cause annihilation.

### 🛠️ Supported Versions

Currently, support is planned for the following versions only:

- **Forge 1.20.1**
- **NeoForge 1.21.1**

### 🙏 Acknowledgements

Many of the ideas and inspirations behind this mod come from the community. Thanks to everyone whose ideas have helped shape this project!

If you have any good ideas, feel free to share them with the author in any way you can think of.

## 🇨🇳 中文

Minecraft 中的一些特性或玩法可能不符合常理，或与你的预期有所不同。**反夸克（Anti-Quark）** 引入了一系列新的特性、玩法机制与配置选项，为游戏带来一些出乎意料的变化。

作者认为大多数玩家都能接受的内容将默认启用。你也可以根据自己的喜好调整配置，自由决定启用哪些特性。

### ✨ 特性

以下仅展示部分特性。

- **狼会猎杀骷髅马** —— 未驯服的狼会把骷髅马当作猎物。
- **无畏的猫** —— 30% 自然生成的猫不再害怕玩家。
- **虚空不死图腾** —— 手持不死图腾坠入虚空时会触发图腾效果并送到安全落脚点。
- **彩虹羊毛** —— 剪命名为 `jeb_` 的羊会掉落会变色的彩虹羊毛。
- **骑乘时挖掘不减速** —— 在船或矿车上挖掘的速度与地面一致。
- **拴绳拴住任意生物** —— 生存模式下拴绳可以拴住任意生物。

完整列表（含默认状态与可调概率）见 **[FEATURES.zh_CN.md](https://github.com/Huziyang520/AntiQuark/blob/master/FEATURES.zh_CN.md)**。
