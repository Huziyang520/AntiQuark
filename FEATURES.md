# Anti-Quark · Features

## Creatures

- **Wolf Targets Skeleton Horses** — Untamed wolves treat skeleton horses as valid prey. Tamed wolves are
  unaffected. *Enabled by default.*
- **Wolves Hunt Players** — A configurable share of newly spawned untamed wolves treat players as prey.
  The chance is configurable; the default value of 0 keeps the feature dormant. *Enabled by default.*
- **Fearless Cats** — 30% of naturally spawned cats no longer flee from players. The chance is configurable.
  *Enabled by default.*
- **Hostile Mobs Cannot Ride** — Hostile mobs cannot board boats or minecarts. *Disabled by default.*
- **Passive Mobs Cannot Ride** — Passive animals cannot board boats or minecarts. *Disabled by default.*

## Gameplay

- **Leash Any Mob** — In survival mode a lead can be attached to any mob. Villagers are handled by their own
  option instead. *Enabled by default.*
- **Leash Villagers** — Villagers can be leashed. *Disabled by default.*
- **Void Totem** — Holding a totem of undying while falling into the void triggers the totem effect and
  teleports you to a safe landing spot within 128 blocks horizontally. When no safe spot exists, the totem
  neither triggers nor is consumed. *Enabled by default.*
- **Guaranteed Zombie Villagers** — Zombies convert villagers into zombie villagers on any non-peaceful
  difficulty instead of only normal and hard. *Enabled by default.*
- **Mining While Riding** — Mining from a boat or a minecart is as fast as mining on foot. *Enabled by default.*
- **Rainbow Wool** — Shearing a sheep named `jeb_` drops **rainbow wool** blocks, whose colour keeps shifting
  wherever they are placed. *Enabled by default.*
- **Flour** — Wheat grinds into 2 flour, and flour with lime dye crafts back into a slime ball.
  *Enabled by default.*
- **Grass to Green Dye** — Nine of any grass (grass, fern, tall grass, large fern) craft into 2 green dye.
  *Enabled by default.*
